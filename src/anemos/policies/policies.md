## Check-in and check-out
<!-- policy_id: check-in-check-out -->
Check-in is from 15:00 to 22:00. Check-out is by 11:00 on the day you leave.

## Arriving late
<!-- policy_id: late-arrival -->
Reception is open for check-in until 22:00. If you expect to arrive later than 22:00, please tell us before you travel, and we will reply to let you know what we can arrange.

## Early check-in and late check-out
<!-- policy_id: early-check-in-late-check-out -->
Early check-in and late check-out are possible on request, depending on the day's bookings. We can only confirm them on the day itself.

## Luggage storage
<!-- policy_id: luggage-storage -->
We store your luggage free of charge on the day you arrive and on the day you leave, before check-in and after check-out.

## Cancellation
<!-- policy_id: cancellation -->
Cancellation is free of charge up to 14 days before your arrival date. Free cancellation is up to 30 days before arrival for arrivals in high season. The season of your arrival date decides which period applies to the whole stay. After the free-cancellation period, the first night is charged.

## Deposit
<!-- policy_id: deposit -->
To confirm a booking we ask for a deposit of 30% of the accommodation total, rounded up to the whole euro. When we hold a room for you, the deposit is due within 48 hours. The city tax is not part of the deposit.

## Holding a room and confirming a booking
<!-- policy_id: holds-and-confirmation -->
When we offer you exactly the room and dates you asked for, we can hold the room for you for 48 hours. A held room is not yet a booking: the booking is confirmed once we have received your deposit. If the deposit does not arrive in time, the hold ends and the room becomes free again.

## Payment methods
<!-- policy_id: payment-methods -->
You can pay the deposit by bank transfer or by card. The rest of the stay is paid at the hotel, by card or in cash.

## City tax
<!-- policy_id: city-tax -->
The local city tax is €1.50 per room per night in low season and €4.00 per room per night in mid and high season. It is paid at the hotel on arrival and is not included in the accommodation total or in the deposit.

## Breakfast
<!-- policy_id: breakfast -->
Breakfast is included for all guests and is served every morning from 08:00 to 10:30.

## Pets and assistance dogs
<!-- policy_id: pets-assistance-dogs -->
We are sorry, but pets are not allowed at the hotel. Assistance dogs are welcome.

## Transfer
<!-- policy_id: transfer -->
We can arrange a transfer between the hotel and the port or the island's airport. It costs €25.00 per trip per car, for up to 4 passengers with their luggage.

## Wi-Fi
<!-- policy_id: wifi -->
Wi-Fi is free in all rooms and public areas.

## Parking
<!-- policy_id: parking -->
The hotel has no car park of its own. There is free public parking 100 m from the hotel.

## Smoking
<!-- policy_id: smoking -->
Smoking is not allowed indoors, including in the rooms. You are welcome to smoke in the garden.

## Standard Double room
<!-- policy_id: room-standard-double -->
Our Standard Double rooms look onto the garden and sleep up to 2 guests. A baby cot can be added; there is no room for an extra bed.

## Sea View Double room
<!-- policy_id: room-sea-view-double -->
Our Sea View Double rooms have a balcony with a view of the sea and sleep up to 3 guests: 2 in the double bed and 1 in an extra bed.

## Family Suite
<!-- policy_id: room-family-suite -->
Our Family Suites have a kitchenette and sleep up to 4 guests: 2 in the double bed and the 3rd and 4th guest on sofa beds, which are charged as extra beds.

## Accessible Double room
<!-- policy_id: room-accessible-double -->
Our Accessible Double room is step-free and sleeps up to 2 guests. A baby cot can be added; there is no room for an extra bed.

## Extra beds
<!-- policy_id: extra-beds -->
An extra bed is possible in a Sea View Double room, and the sofa beds in a Family Suite are charged as extra beds. An extra bed costs €20.00 per night for a child aged 2 to 11 and €35.00 per night for a guest aged 12 and over. The first 2 guests in a room, oldest first, use the standard places; extra beds are charged for the youngest guests.

## Cots and infants
<!-- policy_id: cots-and-infants -->
A baby cot is free of charge for children under 2. A child under 2 sleeping in a cot does not count towards the number of guests a room can take.

## Children
<!-- policy_id: children -->
Children are very welcome. When you ask for a quote, please tell us the age of each child, so we can choose the right room and price. Children under 18 stay in a room with at least one adult.

## Accessibility
<!-- policy_id: accessibility -->
Our Accessible Double room is step-free. If you or someone in your party has access needs, please tell us when you write, and we will reply about what we can arrange for you.

## Groups
<!-- policy_id: groups -->
For bookings of 3 or more rooms, the owner replies to you personally.

## Opening season and seasons
<!-- policy_id: season-and-opening -->
The hotel is open from 15 April to 31 October. Low season is 15 April to 31 May and 1 October to 31 October. Mid season is 1 June to 14 July and 1 September to 30 September. High season is 15 July to 31 August.

## Minimum stay
<!-- policy_id: minimum-stay -->
The minimum stay depends on your arrival date: 1 night in low season, 2 nights in mid season and 3 nights in high season. For arrivals from 10 to 20 August the minimum stay is 5 nights.

## Changing or cancelling a booking
<!-- policy_id: changes-and-cancellation-requests -->
To change or cancel an existing booking, please write to us. The owner replies to you personally.
